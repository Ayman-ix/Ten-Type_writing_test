About TenType

TenType is a minimal, keyboard-first typing test. Pick a duration, start typing, and watch your words‑per‑minute and accuracy update as you go — no clicking required once you've started.

Your results and personal bests are kept on this device only, using your browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere in this version.
